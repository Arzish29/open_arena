    Three ways to control a game

    Every game in the cabinet uses one of these. Pick a game below and the screen jumps to it.

    Your hands

    The webcam feeds a hand-landmark model that returns 21 points per hand. We turn those points into poses: pinch, open palm, fist and swipe. Each pose maps to a game input.

    Your voice

    The microphone goes two ways. Speech-to-text turns words like left and up into commands, and a loudness meter turns volume into thrust.

    Your notes

    Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) lets the quiz games use your own material. Drop in lecture notes or a PDF, and an LLM writes questions from the passages it finds, with the page each one came from.

    How a quiz question gets written

    Quiz Duel and Doc Boss share one engine. This is what runs between your upload and the first question.

    1. Upload

      Notes, slides or a PDF go in.

    2. Split

      The text is cut into passages of about 200 words with a small overlap.

    3. Embed

      Each passage becomes a vector and goes into the index.

    4. Retrieve

      The chosen topic is embedded the same way. The closest passages come back.

    5. Write

      The LLM writes a question and four options from those passages and names the page it used.
